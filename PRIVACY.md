# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**LowFreq (低频猎手)** — last updated 2026-10-21

## English

LowFreq does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **Microphone**: the app uses the microphone only to analyze the frequency and strength of sound in real time, on your device. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
- **Saved spots**: the names and readings of spots you measure are stored only on your device, and are removed when you delete the app.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no network requests.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/zjywill/NoiseMonitor/issues

## 中文

低频猎手不收集、不存储、不分享任何个人数据。

- **麦克风**：只用来在手机上实时分析声音的频率和强度，不录音、不保存、不上传。
- **位置记录**：你测量的位置名称和读数只保存在本机，删除 App 即清除。
- **没有账号、没有统计、没有广告、没有追踪，也不联网。**

有问题请在 https://github.com/zjywill/NoiseMonitor/issues 提 issue。
